Scoring Tiers

Change the maths and generate again to see what happens. Sale's tier scoring is used by Sale's generator, and by the Draft Scorer when it splits with Sale's scoring. Each tier defines a minimum win rate and the points awarded. Tiers are checked from top to bottom — the first one the win rate meets or exceeds wins. Anything below the lowest tier scores 0.

Confidence Modifier

If a matchup has fewer than X games played, multiply the points scored by coefficient Y (0–1). Set Y=0 to ignore low-sample matchups entirely. Applies to tier scoring only.

Komarica's Linear Scoring

Used by Komarica's and the Fair-enough generator, by Pure random, MBMP and Land of Unknown to split their tiers, and by the Draft Scorer when it splits with Komarica's scoring. A matchup is worth (win rate − 50%) / A points, times games / (games + B).

MBMP & Land of Unknown

MBMP (most balanced, most played): every matchup between 40% and 60% is worth log(1 + games played), times the bonus between 45% and 55%; the draft with the highest total wins. Land of Unknown: the draft with the fewest games played between its heroes wins.
